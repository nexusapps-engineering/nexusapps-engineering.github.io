Before we start

A few useful answers

Can you build for both iOS and Android?
Yes. We discuss your audience, platform requirements and budget before agreeing on a native or shared implementation approach. Our current public app examples are on the Apple App Store.
What determines the cost and timeline?
Platform count, screen complexity, integrations, accounts, payments and backend requirements all affect the scope. We agree on deliverables and a release plan before development; there is no universal price or deadline.
Can you work on an existing app?
We can discuss an existing product after reviewing its code, design and current requirements. That review determines what can be reused and what needs to change.
What happens after launch?
Maintenance, release updates and new features can be agreed as an ongoing engagement. The scope and responsibilities are made explicit rather than assumed.
