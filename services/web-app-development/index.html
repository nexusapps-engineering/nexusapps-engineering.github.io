Before we start

Questions about the work

What kinds of web products can we discuss?
SaaS applications, dashboards, customer portals and internal tools. We start with the workflow and users, then agree on the product scope.
Can the product connect to our existing systems?
We assess the available APIs, access requirements and data flows during discovery. We confirm feasibility and scope before including an integration in the build.
How do you choose the technology?
We choose the technology around your product requirements, integrations, expected usage and the team that will maintain it.
Can you improve the product after release?
Yes. A continuing engagement can cover maintenance, new workflows and product improvements, with priorities and responsibilities agreed together.
